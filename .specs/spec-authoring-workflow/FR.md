# Functional Requirements

All runtime identities are qualified as `spec-authoring-workflow:FR-N`. These requirements define a deferred, unregistered authoring release; implementation and evidence production may proceed before registration eligibility is proven.

## FR-1: Deferred eligibility and single-plugin boundary

The product SHALL expose or register no authoring action and SHALL permit no mutation of user specifications until the all-of gate in FR-13 is satisfied. While lifecycle is `DEFERRED`, maintainers MAY implement and internally verify the versioned schema, shared service, root/transaction/recovery authorities, real fixtures, and pure eligibility evaluator against isolated test inputs; that work MAY create candidate-bound evidence but SHALL NOT register an action, mutate a user's specification, claim release eligibility, or bypass any dependency gate. Eligibility SHALL follow `DEFERRED → ELIGIBLE → IMPLEMENTED → PROVEN`: FR-13 alone permits `DEFERRED→ELIGIBLE` for registration/release consideration, registration of the gated existing extension permits `ELIGIBLE→IMPLEMENTED`, and exact installed-artifact evidence permits `IMPLEMENTED→PROVEN`. Evidence regression returns any non-deferred state to `DEFERRED`. Implementation SHALL use the existing `plugins/omp-spec-kit` package and its single extension entry.

**Contract card:** kind `state`; subject `authoring-release-eligibility`; observables: lifecycle state, implementation-evidence identity, separately qualified v0.2/v0.3 kernel evidence identities and linkage, distribution identity, registration state, next action; negative cases: treating deferred as an implementation ban, exposing a deferred service, missing/stale/revoked/duplicate-stage/cross-lineage proof, second plugin/extension; verification: isolated pre-registration implementation evidence plus linked-kernel-profile and installed-artifact behavioral tests, pending.

## FR-2: Proposal and bounded diff preview

Every prospective content change SHALL first produce a read-only proposal from one immutable kernel snapshot. A proposal SHALL include `proposalId`, `proposalHash`, base snapshot identity, per-document expected/current/result hashes, normalized operations, bounded unified diffs, affected canonical nodes, validation findings, creation/expiry, actor, reason, and eligibility state. Proposal creation SHALL write neither target documents nor transaction/audit state. Preview bounds SHALL report exact excess; a truncated proposal SHALL not be reviewable or applicable, and the caller SHALL split or reduce the request and create a fresh complete proposal. An authenticated caller SHALL explicitly review the complete proposal ID/hash before any apply; `apply_transaction` SHALL accept no raw edit operations and SHALL consume only that reviewed, unexpired proposal plus current expected document and base-snapshot hashes.

Proposal states SHALL be exactly `CREATED`, `VALIDATED`, `REJECTED`, `REVIEWED`, `STALE`, `APPLYING`, `COMMITTED`, `ROLLED_BACK`, `CANCELLED`, and `EXPIRED`. Transitions: `CREATED→VALIDATED|REJECTED`; `VALIDATED→REVIEWED|STALE|CANCELLED|EXPIRED`; `REVIEWED→APPLYING|STALE|CANCELLED|EXPIRED`; `APPLYING→COMMITTED|ROLLED_BACK`; all other transitions refuse. Terminal states are `REJECTED`, `STALE`, `COMMITTED`, `ROLLED_BACK`, `CANCELLED`, and `EXPIRED`.

**Contract card:** kind `api`; subject `proposal-review-boundary`; requests and responses are defined in the schema; negative cases: invalid edit, bound-exceeded/truncated preview, missing review, expired or mismatched proposal, raw-edit apply; verification: behavioral, review-separation, and hash-conservation tests, pending.

## FR-3: Expected-hash CAS and concurrency

Every `apply_transaction` request SHALL identify an explicitly reviewed, unexpired proposal by `proposalId` and `proposalHash` and provide the current expected SHA-256 of every document it may change plus the base snapshot hash. Under a root-scoped exclusive mutation lease, the service SHALL compare the proposal identity and all expected values before staging and again immediately before the generation swap. One mismatch marks the proposal `STALE` or returns `HASH_MISMATCH`; it SHALL never auto-rebase or overwrite in the first authoring release. Equal request replay SHALL return the prior terminal result by `requestId` and SHALL not duplicate a commit.

**Contract card:** kind `behavior`; subject `compare-and-swap`; observables: one winner, explicit loser, unchanged non-winner bytes; negative cases: ABA content change, lease timeout, replay; verification: deterministic race test, pending.
**Acceptance:** [AC-3.1](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-31), [AC-3.2](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-32), [AC-3.3](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-33)

## FR-4: Validation before write

The service SHALL apply normalized edits to an in-memory copy and run, in order: request/schema validation; root/target validation; canonical-document policy; parse and identity collision checks; form checks; anchor and inbound-link closure; FR↔AC↔scenario↔task traceability; status guards; and authoritative kernel conformance. Any error, unavailable mandatory validator, or snapshot drift SHALL refuse before stage creation. The exact same validator set and policy version SHALL run over the staged generation before commit. Structural success alone SHALL not mean ready or valid.

**Contract card:** kind `behavior`; subject `pre-write-validation`; observables: ordered lanes, findings, zero stage on refusal; negative cases: unavailable validator, planted broken link, trace loss; verification: fault-seeded behavioral tests, pending.
**Acceptance:** [AC-4.1](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-41), [AC-4.2](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-42), [AC-4.3](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-43)

## FR-5: Root containment and deny-by-default symlink policy

A request SHALL use one explicitly selected canonical repository root and root-relative targets matching `.specs/<slug>/<canonical-document>`. The selected root and every component to the spec directory and target SHALL be rejected if it is a symlink, junction, mount point, or other reparse point. A linked spec directory is unsupported for kernel reading, proposal construction, validation, and mutation; containment SHALL refuse before reading its document content. The resolver SHALL reject empty/dot/dotdot segments, traversal, absolute, drive-relative, UNC, device paths, alternate data streams, NUL, unsupported documents, normalized case/Unicode collisions, and resolved paths outside the root. Existing ancestors SHALL be realpath-checked; a future target's nearest existing ancestor SHALL be confined before creation. No symlink allowlist or follow mode exists in this release.

**Contract card:** kind `filesystem`; subject `target-containment`; artifacts: canonical spec documents; atomicity: delegated to FR-6; rollback: zero write on resolution error; negative cases: traversal and reparse escape; verification: cross-platform filesystem fixtures, pending.
**Acceptance:** [AC-5.1](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-51), [AC-5.2](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-52), [AC-5.3](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-53), [AC-5.4](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-54)

## FR-6: Atomic same-spec transaction, rollback, and recovery

A transaction SHALL consume one explicitly reviewed proposal containing one or more document operations for exactly one spec. Cross-spec, duplicate-target, raw-edit, unreviewed, expired, and preview-truncated transactions SHALL refuse. After proposal identity verification, CAS, and validation, the writer SHALL stage a complete copy of the spec generation on the same filesystem, record content hashes in a minimal transient journal, fsync files/directories where supported, and swap generations while holding the exclusive coordinator lease; kernel readers SHALL use the matching shared coordinator and observe only committed generations.

Transaction states SHALL be `PREPARING`, `PREPARED`, `COMMITTING`, `COMMITTED`, `ROLLING_BACK`, `ROLLED_BACK`, `RECOVERING`, `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`, `REBASELINING`, `REBASELINED`, or `FAILED_PREPARE`. Transitions: `PREPARING→PREPARED|FAILED_PREPARE`; `PREPARED→COMMITTING|ROLLING_BACK`; `COMMITTING→COMMITTED|ROLLING_BACK|RECOVERING`; `ROLLING_BACK→ROLLED_BACK|RECOVERY_REQUIRED`; and `RECOVERING→COMMITTED|ROLLED_BACK|RECOVERY_REQUIRED`. Deterministic recovery or authenticated `recover_transaction` SHALL prove and select a complete retained original or result generation, transitioning `RECOVERY_REQUIRED→RECOVERING→COMMITTED|ROLLED_BACK|RECOVERY_REQUIRED`. If and only if neither retained generation is complete and valid, bounded `propose_rebaseline_recovery` MAY read an operator-selected, root-contained, ordinary unlinked candidate directory without writing, verify an unexpired host authorization bound to actor/transaction/root/candidate and at most the canonical 15 documents, compare the expected current snapshot/document hashes and exact retained journal or canonical missing-journal marker hash, run every mandatory validator, and return a complete pre/post hash proposal. A separately reviewed exact proposal MAY be consumed by `apply_rebaseline_recovery`, which reacquires the exclusive lease, rechecks authorization, proposal/current/journal/candidate hashes, containment, link closure, validator output, audit-chain continuity, and concurrent state before atomically installing one complete new generation through `RECOVERY_REQUIRED→REBASELINING→REBASELINED`. Requests contain only candidate locators and hashes, never document bytes. Any authorization, bound, mismatch, leak/linked-path, validation, audit, or concurrency error SHALL remain `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`, preserve the blocked current bytes, journal, retained recovery material, candidate source, and append-only audit history, and keep normal reads/writes blocked. Rebaseline SHALL append a hash-bound event and SHALL NOT rewrite or erase the failed transaction or recovery history. Only `COMMITTED`, `ROLLED_BACK`, `REBASELINED`, and `FAILED_PREPARE` permit normal subsequent writes. A crash SHALL trigger mandatory deterministic recovery before reads or writes proceed.

**Contract card:** kind `filesystem`; subject `same-spec-atomic-transaction`; atomicity: generation swap under coordinator; rollback: preimage restoration, authenticated retained-generation selection, or authenticated proposal-before-write rebaseline when neither retained original nor retained result is complete and valid, even if corrupt or incomplete retained directories exist; negative cases: stage/write/swap/audit/recovery fault, raw/unreviewed apply, invalid recovery authorization/selection, rebaseline current/journal/candidate mismatch, linked/leaking candidate, validation/audit/concurrency failure, history erasure; verification: fault injection at every boundary plus concurrent reader/writer, retained-generation recovery, and no-survivor rebaseline tests, pending.
**Acceptance:** [AC-6.1](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-61), [AC-6.2](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-62), [AC-6.3](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-63), [AC-6.4](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-64), [AC-6.5](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-65), [AC-6.6](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-66), [AC-6.7](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-67), [AC-6.8](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-68)

## FR-7: Anchor-safe editing

Edit operations SHALL address a unique heading identity and optional section hash, not an incidental line number. Supported operations SHALL be `replace_document`, `replace_section`, `insert_after_heading`, `append_to_section`, and `rename_heading`. They SHALL preserve source EOL style and untouched bytes. Safe rename SHALL depend on the authoritative `spec-kernel:FR-13` query over one immutable repository snapshot, including the complete heading-definition, generated-anchor, and link-occurrence inventory. A heading rename SHALL calculate the kernel's canonical Markdown slug and include all same-spec inbound rewrites in the proposal. A cross-spec inbound link, linked spec directory, duplicate/ambiguous heading, changed section hash, incomplete inventory, or generated slug collision SHALL refuse; no heuristic or model-selected target is allowed.

**Contract card:** kind `behavior`; subject `anchor-safe-edit`; observables: unique selector, byte preservation, link closure; negative cases: ambiguity, external inbound, concurrent section change; verification: captured Markdown/anchor fixtures and mutation tests, pending.
**Acceptance:** [AC-7.1](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-71), [AC-7.2](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-72), [AC-7.3](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-73)

## FR-8: Guarded task/status lifecycle

Stored task statuses SHALL be exactly `todo`, `ready`, `in-progress`, `blocked`, and `done`. Legal transitions SHALL be `todo→ready|blocked`, `ready→in-progress|blocked|todo`, `in-progress→blocked|done|ready`, `blocked→todo|ready|in-progress`, and `done→in-progress`; all others refuse. `ready` requires a current FR, AC, scenario, task, design, and research trace. `in-progress` requires the same assembled chain and an owner. `done` requires all Done When items checked plus current task-owned strong evidence for every linked scenario; missing, stale, filtered-only, weak, skipped, undefined, ambiguous, or failed evidence refuses. `done→in-progress` is the only reopen transition. Status requests SHALL use `TASKS.md` expected hash and the FR-2/FR-6 path; raw text edits that change status without a valid transition SHALL fail validation.

**Contract card:** kind `state`; subject `task-status`; states/transitions/guards are exhaustive above; terminal outcome: none, because done is reopenable; negative cases: illegal edge and stale evidence; verification: transition matrix and concurrency tests, pending.
**Acceptance:** [AC-8.1](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-81), [AC-8.2](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-82), [AC-8.3](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-83), [AC-8.4](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-84), [AC-8.5](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-85)

## FR-9: Provenance and redacted audit evidence

Every proposal, explicit review, refusal, apply, rollback, automatic/manual retained-generation recovery, rebaseline proposal/review/apply/refusal, cancel, and status transition SHALL return an `AuditEnvelope` containing schema/policy version, event and request/transaction/proposal IDs, timestamp, actor identity reference, non-empty reason, canonical root fingerprint, artifact/plugin/kernel snapshot identity, operations, complete pre/post or selected-generation hashes, current/journal/candidate fingerprints where applicable, findings, outcome, rollback/recovery state, and previous-event digest. It SHALL exclude raw document/diff content, credentials, environment values, recovery authorization material, retained/candidate bytes, and unrelated paths. Rebaseline audit SHALL append to rather than rewrite or erase failed-transaction history. The service SHALL not create persistent audit or workflow state inside the user's repository. Optional durable export requires explicit configuration and redaction; sink failure before commit aborts, while sink failure after a proven commit returns `COMMITTED_WITH_AUDIT_EXPORT_FAILURE` and never misreports user bytes as unchanged.

**Contract card:** kind `event`; subject `authoring-audit`; ordering: hash-chained per root; retry: idempotent by event ID; duplicate: returns existing envelope; negative cases: secret-like content and sink failure; verification: schema, redaction, and replay tests, pending.
**Acceptance:** [AC-9.1](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-91), [AC-9.2](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-92), [AC-9.3](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-93)

## FR-11: Mutation-resistance release gate

Release SHALL be blocked until behavioral tests kill 100% of mutants in these safety-critical families: lifecycle/all-of eligibility bypass; deferred-implementation mistaken for registration or mutation authority; omitted proposal-review transition; raw-edit or same-call preview-and-commit acceptance; proposal ID/hash/expiry mismatch bypass; omitted expected-hash comparison; compare-after-write; lease bypass; replay duplication; validation-lane omission or severity downgrade; root/traversal/absolute/UNC/device check removal; linked/symlink/junction/reparse read-or-mutation check removal; normalization collision removal; heading/anchor/link-occurrence inventory incompleteness or ambiguity bypass; partial-commit exposure; rollback/preimage restoration removal; recovery-block bypass; retained-recovery authorization/bound/selected-hash/validation bypass; no-survivor precondition bypass; rebaseline authorization/proposal/current-hash/journal-hash/candidate-hash/containment/link/validation/concurrency/audit-history bypass; illegal status-transition/guard/review bypass; audit redaction removal; and error-to-success inversion. `NO_COVERAGE`, `SURVIVED`, `TIMED_OUT`, `SKIPPED`, runner error, missing expected mutant family, or nondeterministic inventory SHALL block. Baseline and post-restore behavioral runs SHALL be green. General-code threshold, engine, timeout, and equivalent-mutant authority remain `DECISION_REQUIRED` as recorded in RESEARCH.md and therefore keep FR-13 deferred without preventing implementation and evidence production.

**Contract card:** kind `behavior`; subject `mutation-release-gate`; observables: complete critical inventory and per-mutant evidence; negative cases: survivor, missing family, timeout, flaky inventory; verification: real mutation engine against built artifact, pending.
**Acceptance:** [AC-11.1](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-111), [AC-11.2](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-112), [AC-11.3](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-113), [AC-11.4](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-114)

## FR-13: Aggregate authoring eligibility

FR-13 SHALL govern authoring action registration and release eligibility, not the start of schema, service, fixture, evaluator, or test implementation. Authoring SHALL advance from `DEFERRED` to `ELIGIBLE` only when one all-of evaluation proves: current mandatory evidence for every `spec-authoring-workflow:FR-1` through `spec-authoring-workflow:FR-12`; current accepted `plugin-distribution:FR-13`; and exactly two distinct accepted `spec-kernel:FR-14` `kernel-release-eligibility@1` envelopes, one qualified as `targetStage: "v0.2"` / `evidenceProfile: "kernel-v0.2"` and one qualified as `targetStage: "v0.3"` / `evidenceProfile: "kernel-v0.3"`. The v0.2 envelope SHALL be `eligible: true`, current, and not revoked, with artifact SHA-256 `A` and `v02ParentArtifactSha256: null`; the v0.3 envelope SHALL be `eligible: true`, current, and not revoked, with artifact SHA-256 `B` and `v02ParentArtifactSha256: A`. `A` and `B` MAY differ: acceptance depends on that cryptographic predecessor/current link, not hash equality. Both kernel envelopes SHALL carry the same product revision and artifact-lineage identity as the authoring release evaluation; the v0.3 envelope, current distribution aggregate, and FR-1..FR-12 evidence SHALL bind to the current built release candidate, kernel snapshot, policy version, and supported host.

Evidence SHALL be enumerated per qualified requirement and, for `spec-kernel:FR-14`, per target stage. One unqualified kernel envelope, two envelopes for the same target stage, a v0.3 envelope substituted for the v0.2 envelope, a stale or revoked v0.2 envelope, a non-eligible envelope, or a v0.3 parent hash that does not equal the accepted v0.2 artifact hash SHALL fail closed. An aggregate count, partial subset, any-of match, inherited green state, source-tree-only result, unrelated parent/current pair, or differently versioned proof SHALL not satisfy the gate. One missing, stale, red, revoked, ambiguous, duplicate, or mismatched mandatory envelope SHALL keep or return authoring to `DEFERRED` and keep every authoring action unregistered, but SHALL NOT invalidate honest candidate-bound implementation evidence or prohibit further implementation. Authoring eligibility SHALL NOT independently authorize publication or override pending public-init validation or the fail-closed provenance/license policy for future or changed imports carried by `plugin-distribution:FR-13`; registration and release remain subject to the applicable distribution gate.

**Contract card:** kind `state`; subject `aggregate-authoring-eligibility`; aggregation: all mandatory evidence for FR-1..FR-12, one current distribution aggregate, and exactly one accepted kernel aggregate for each target stage v0.2 and v0.3; observables: every qualified evidence identity, both kernel target stages/profiles, v0.2 artifact hash, v0.3 artifact and parent hashes, shared product revision/artifact lineage, release-candidate artifact/snapshot/policy binding, missing/stale/revoked/red/duplicate/mismatched set, registration state, next action; negative cases: blocking implementation start, registering while deferred, one omitted requirement, unqualified or duplicate-stage kernel evidence, v0.3-for-v0.2 substitution, stale/revoked parent, parent-hash mismatch, any-of/count-only evaluation, version mismatch; verification: pre-registration evaluator tests, planted single-envelope removal, stage-duplicate and lineage one-fault matrices, and exact-artifact installed lifecycle tests, pending.
**Acceptance:** [AC-13.1](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-131), [AC-13.2](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-132), [AC-13.3](ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md#ac-133)
